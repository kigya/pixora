Privacy Policy for Pixora

Effective date: 11 October 2026 · Last updated: 22 October 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Kirill Borichevskiy ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with Pixora for Android and iOS (Android package and iOS bundle identifier dev.kigya.pixora). Pixora enlarges photos and illustrations and offers experimental image recreation on your device.

Pixora has no account, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. Image enlargement runs on the device. The enlargement models are included in the app. The optional recreation models are downloaded only after you choose Download model and accept their terms.

Photos stay on your device

You choose one image with the system photo picker. Pixora processes that image on the device and can save a new copy to your gallery or hand it to another app that you pick in the share sheet.

Pixora does not upload photos, file names, file locations, or location data stored inside a photo. It does not copy GPS or other EXIF data into the saved result. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

Information that leaves the device

Some information is sent off the device, as described below. It is sent over HTTPS.

Optional recreation-model download

Experimental recreation uses two model files totaling 1,881,812,340 bytes, downloaded from Hugging Face over HTTPS after your explicit action. Hugging Face and its delivery providers receive your network IP address and ordinary HTTP request information, including the requested model file and a Pixora app user agent. The request contains no photo, photo filename, local file path, description, seed, or account token. See Hugging Face's privacy policy.

Downloads can be cancelled and resumed. Pixora checks pinned SHA-256 hashes before using the files. The models stay in private app storage until you choose Delete model or uninstall the app. Clearing the cache does not delete these models. Recreation itself works locally, including any optional description you enter. The description and processing parameters remain in the local session and are not sent to analytics or a server.

Usage analytics and error reports

The release app sends usage analytics and error reports to Google Firebase by default. You can turn Usage analytics and Error reports off separately in Settings. Turning them off does not turn off the availability check described below. The first launch shows a short notice about this. Debug builds do not send them by default.

Usage analytics can include which screens and features are used, the chosen processing mode, speed, scale, whether processing used the GPU or CPU, a size category of the image, success or failure, durations, export format, and similar product settings. Error reports can include crash details, device model, operating system, and app version.

These reports do not include photos, file names, file locations, email addresses, or the text of a support message. Pixora does not collect the Android advertising ID, and it does not use analytics for ads or personalization.

Firebase may also receive an app instance identifier, device and app information, and an approximate region derived from the network connection. Google's own policy is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Which processing modes are available

When the phone has an internet connection, Pixora asks Firebase Remote Config which processing modes are available. This check sends a Firebase installation identifier and technical information such as country code, language, time zone, operating-system version and app version. It does not include your photo or support message. Without a connection, the app uses the last saved availability, or enables every mode if it has never connected. Turning off analytics or error reports does not turn off this check.

Support messages

A support message is sent only when you tap Send. Pixora then sends:

  • your email address, only if you enter one, so we can reply;
  • your message;
  • the app version and platform (Android or iOS);
  • a random request id.

The current support form does not ask for a rating. Earlier app versions may also send the optional 1–5 support rating they requested.

The message goes through a Google Apps Script web app to kirillborichevskiy@gmail.com. It does not include a photo, a file name, or a device identifier. Google's policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Notifications

On Android, Pixora can show a notification while an image is being processed. On iOS, Pixora can request notification permission and show a local completion notification. The notification permission is optional. Refusing it does not stop processing. These notifications are not marketing messages.

Who receives information

We do not sell personal information.

  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config) receives the usage, error, and availability information described above. On Android, Firebase uses Google Play services where applicable; iOS uses the Firebase Apple SDKs.
  • Hugging Face and its delivery providers serve the optional recreation model files only after you request a download.
  • Google Apps Script delivers a support message to us only after you tap Send.
  • Kirill Borichevskiy receives that support message by email and uses it to reply.

The Google Analytics account has Google products and services data sharing turned off. Modelling contributions and business insights are enabled and use aggregated, de-identified measurement data. Google technical support access and account-level business recommendations are enabled. These settings do not add photos or support-message text to analytics.

A share sheet sends the image file only to the app you choose. That transfer is made by you, not by Pixora's analytics.

How long information is kept

Photos and working files stay on your phone. You can delete the current session or temporary files in the app, and you can limit how much working data the app keeps between launches. Files you save to the gallery are not deleted by that cleanup.

To limit duplicate requests and abuse, the support service temporarily caches a random request ID for up to 6 hours and, when an email is supplied, a hash of the normalized email address for up to 10 minutes. Google may evict cache entries earlier. Support messages are kept for as long as needed to reply and handle follow-up. As checked on 17 October 2026, the Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. The user-data retention period resets on new activity. These settings do not govern most aggregated standard reports. Google states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Remote Config installation identifiers are retained until a deletion API request; Google then removes them within 180 days. Pixora does not currently offer an in-app request to delete all server-side installation data. See Firebase's retention details. You can ask us to delete a support message we still hold.

Your choices

  • Turn Usage analytics and Error reports on or off in Settings. Each switch is independent.
  • Use enlargement without downloading the recreation models. Delete downloaded models separately in Settings.
  • Do not send a support message. Nothing from that form is sent until you tap Send.
  • Refuse or revoke the notification permission in your device settings.
  • Email kirillborichevskiy@gmail.com to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of a support message we hold. Pixora cannot delete a photo from your phone, because the photo is not uploaded.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to restrict or object to certain processing and to complain to a supervisory authority. If you are a California resident, you may have the right to know and delete personal information, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Pixora does not sell personal information and does not share it for cross-context advertising.

Children

Pixora is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum age in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us a support message, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and international transfers

Information that leaves the device is sent over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Google may process Firebase data and a support delivery in countries other than yours. Where the law requires it, those providers use their own transfer safeguards.

Opening this policy

The full policy opens in your browser only when you choose its link in Pixora. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. This page contains no analytics scripts or advertising trackers added by us. The link from Pixora contains no photo, filename, email address or other user data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The page will show a new “Last updated” date. If a change is significant, Pixora may also mention it in the app.
